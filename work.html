Work Examples

Automating Manual Tasks

Knowledge base articles and their review dates

Ensuring knowledge base articles are fresh and up-to-date

ProblemAn institution was relying on their staff to review each knowledge base article and set a valid review date value until the next review cycle.

ApproachI designed an iPaaS flow to find all articles with an empty or upcoming review date, notify the article owner to review the article, and set the review date value to one year in advance once completed.

OutcomeRunning once a day, this flow automatically relieves staff of manual tracking and guarantees every knowledge base article stays current.

Ticketing systems and incident communication platforms

Connecting TeamDynamix Work Management and StatusPage

ProblemWhen a major incident occured, an institution would initially create a ticket in Work Management and then manually update their StatusPage with the incident details.

ApproachUtilizing a 'ticket create' webhook in Work Management, I designed an iPaaS flow to be triggered when a major incident ticket is created, gather all of the details from the ticket, and post an incident on StatusPage containing the details from the ticket.

OutcomeBy eliminating manual system-switching and duplicate work, this workflow frees the institution to concentrate on an accurate major incident ticket and resolving the problem.
